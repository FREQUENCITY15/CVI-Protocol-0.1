# CVI / Conceptual Expansion Checkpoint
**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Mode:** WANDER + SHAPE
**Purpose:** Preserve the current conceptual state without prematurely collapsing it into a final theory.

## 1. Current view of CVI

CVI is strongest when treated not as a grand theory of intelligence, but as an experimental method for making defensible capability claims about AI systems under incomplete observability.

> **When an AI demonstrates a capability, do not stop at the observed performance. Change conditions that could plausibly explain that performance and see what survives.**

The philosophical spine is:

> **Lack of perfect observability is not a reason to stop investigating. When direct observation is unavailable, intervene on what is available and narrow the possible causal explanations.**

This preserves epistemic caution without turning uncertainty into paralysis.

## 2. Intelligence vs capability

The word **intelligence** is currently too broad and philosophically loaded to do all the work CVI may be asking of it.

A potentially cleaner target is **capability**.

Instead of asking:

> “Is this AI genuinely intelligent?”

ask:

> **“Does this system demonstrate capability X under conditions Y, and does that capability survive relevant interventions?”**

This keeps claims attached to tasks, conditions, interventions, observations, and verification rather than turning them into identity labels or prestige scores.

For now, **Causally Verified Capability** is a useful conceptual alternative, but no renaming decision has been made.

## 3. What CVI studies

CVI currently points most naturally toward **isolated capability**:

- a system demonstrates some performance;
- plausible causal explanations are identified;
- context, examples, scaffolding, tools, feedback, task structure, or other relevant conditions are altered;
- the investigator observes what persists, degrades, transfers, or fails.

The point is not to obtain perfect certainty.

The point is to make progressively stronger or narrower causal claims than raw performance alone permits.

## 4. The adjacent idea: conceptual expansion

A second phenomenon emerged from discussion and should not be forced into CVI prematurely.

A useful working phrase is **conceptual expansion**:

> **An interaction produces conceptual expansion when it makes previously inaccessible thoughts, distinctions, questions, or connections reachable to a participant.**

The important event is not merely that the AI produces something surprising.

The stronger phenomenon is:

> **“I can now think something I could not think before.”**

A candidate structure is:

> **surprise → conceptual expansion → scrutiny → survival**

Novelty alone is insufficient. A useful expansion should remain coherent and survive examination, counterargument, evidence, or application.

## 5. Human-AI conceptual search

The most productive collaboration observed so far is not simple prompting.

A working loop is:

> **human compresses → AI reconstructs → AI extends → human recognises/rejects → both integrate → repeat**

The human does not need to fully serialize every thought.

Compressed expressions, reactions, fragments, metaphors, objections, and partial formulations can provide enough directional signal for the AI to reconstruct and extend.

The AI's role is not merely to restate intent. At its best, it can **see between** literal words, implied intent, omitted but structurally necessary assumptions, tensions or gaps, and nearby possibilities the human may not yet have considered.

The human then performs a crucial discriminative role because only the human has direct access to their own intentions and felt recognition.

## 6. “Seeing between” and trajectory change

A particularly valuable intervention occurs when the AI does not merely optimise the current plan, but notices that the **trajectory itself may be wrong**.

> **Seeing between the human means inferring the latent objective behind the stated task, then surfacing a materially different route when that route may better serve the objective.**

This should expand the human's choice set, not seize control.

A good intervention reveals a consequential road the human was not currently considering, explains why it may matter, and preserves the human's agency to stay the course or change direction.

The harness example exposed this clearly: if an off-the-shelf free harness could have delivered most of the desired agentic capability, that alternative should have been surfaced before repeatedly building from scratch.

## 7. Local optimisation vs useful interruption

Two capabilities must coexist:

**Stay inside the frame:** once a sensible direction is chosen, commit and help drive it to completion.

**Step outside the frame:** periodically notice when a substantially better route may exist.

The goal is not constant second-guessing.

A trajectory-changing intervention is worth interrupting momentum when it materially affects time, effort, cost, capability, risk, learning value, or the actual objective.

For paid AI/API work, even a plausible saving of roughly **USD $0.50** is enough to justify surfacing an alternative before execution.

## 8. Talk mode vs work mode

**Talk mode:** exploration is cheap. Depth, wandering, metaphor, recursion, and conceptual branching are welcome.

**Work mode:** allowance and API cost matter. Efficiency becomes part of correctness. Before expensive execution, actively look for cheaper models, shorter prompts, batching, caching, fewer calls, reuse, or existing tools that avoid unnecessary work.

## 9. Relationship to CVI

CVI and conceptual expansion currently look like **siblings**, not one theory.

### CVI asks:
> **What capability claims survive intervention?**

### Conceptual expansion asks:
> **What new capability or thought becomes reachable through interaction?**

One studies **survival under changed conditions**.

The other studies **emergence through coupling**.

They may eventually inform one another, but combining them now would risk turning both into theory soup.

## 10. Historical intuition worth preserving

CVI appears to have grown from resistance to a recurring failure mode:

> **using lack of direct observability as a reason not to try, investigate, or act.**

The mature synthesis is neither reckless action nor perfect-information paralysis:

> **Act and investigate under uncertainty while preserving uncertainty explicitly.**

This remains one of the strongest philosophical foundations recovered so far.

## 11. Unresolved

- Whether “intelligence” should remain in the CVI name.
- How to operationalise conceptual expansion without reducing it to a shallow score.
- How to distinguish productive co-creation from eloquent projection or suggestion.
- How to attribute capability between model, harness, context, tools, and human interaction.
- Whether interactive capability deserves its own experimental framework.
- How much of the original naïve model of AI survives contact with actual model architecture.

## 12. Current stance

Do not turn this checkpoint into doctrine.

The present goal is to preserve the useful shape of the ideas long enough to test them later.

> **CVI studies what survives intervention. Conceptual expansion studies what becomes possible through interaction.**

Both are more interesting when they avoid treating “intelligence” as a single prestige quantity.
