# CVI Glossary 0.1

## Capability claim
A bounded statement that a specified system can produce a specified class of outcomes under specified conditions.

## System boundary
The components included in the object being evaluated: model, prompt, memory, tools, scaffolding, environment, retry policy, humans, and related state.

## Static performance
Performance measured without the treatment-specific correction loop. It can be substantial and useful; CVI simply does not equate it with demonstrated adaptive correction.

## Intervention
A deliberate change used to distinguish competing explanations for an observed result.

## Causal contingency
In this protocol, whether feedback/outcome is actually caused by the participant's preceding action. This is not by itself a claim of formal causal identification in the statistical sense.

## Sham / non-contingent feedback
Feedback matched to treatment in salience or content but decoupled from the participant's preceding action, used to test whether contingency itself matters.

## Immediate correction
A previously observed failure is behaviorally reduced or removed after feedback or another intervention within the active episode.

## Retention boundary
A declared change in state or time across which a correction is tested for persistence, such as a fresh context or process restart.

## Retention
The corrected behavior remains after crossing the declared retention boundary.

## Transfer
The corrected relation or capability is demonstrated on changed-but-relevant tasks rather than only the practiced instance.

## Hidden verification
A test whose decisive answers, instances, or scoring information are unavailable to the participant during the relevant learning/correction process.

## Independent verifier
A scoring or measurement mechanism not controlled by the system being evaluated. Independence is a spectrum and must be described, not assumed.

## Tool parity
Relevant experimental arms receive equivalent execution/search/verification capability unless tool access itself is the variable under study.

## Ceiling / floor
A task region where nearly everyone succeeds or nearly everyone fails, leaving too little headroom to distinguish effects.

## Measurement vector
A set of separately reported outcomes such as baseline, immediate correction, retention, transfer, verification, and resource use. CVI 0.1 prefers this to a single scalar.

## OBSERVED
Directly supported by recorded experimental evidence or an inspected source.

## INFERRED
Reasoned from the observations but not uniquely demonstrated by them.

## UNRESOLVED
Evidence is insufficient to discriminate live alternatives.

## CVI-supported claim
A bounded capability statement whose reported evidence survives the interventions actually performed, with surviving confounds disclosed. CVI 0.1 intentionally avoids an unqualified universal “CVI verified” badge.
